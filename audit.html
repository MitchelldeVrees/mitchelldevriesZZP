Who this is for

This audit is built for small-to-mid financial firms (20–200 employees) where:

  • Operations, compliance, or research teams spend hours on repetitive document or data work.
  • "We should probably use AI somewhere" has been said in a meeting, but nobody knows where to start.
  • You want a concrete plan from someone who understands both AI engineering and how a financial firm actually runs, not a generic consultant pitching ChatGPT licenses.

Not a fit if: you're a 5,000-person bank, you already have an AI/ML team, or you want strategy slides instead of working solutions.

What you get

A written PDF report, delivered within 7 days, containing:

Workflow map

Three to five processes in your firm where AI can realistically reduce time, errors, or cost.

Ranked opportunities

Each opportunity scored on impact, effort, and risk so you know what to tackle first.

Concrete implementations

For each opportunity: what the solution looks like, what tools or models would power it, what data it needs, and a realistic build estimate.

FAQ

How much of our time does the audit need?

Depending on the size of the company, it can take a total of 4 hours for smaller companies and a few days for larger companies.

That's fine and common. The report is yours. Some clients implement internally, some take a year, some pick one thing and ignore the rest. No strings.

Yes, as a separate fix engagement. But there's no obligation to use me — the report is written so any competent engineering team can execute it.
